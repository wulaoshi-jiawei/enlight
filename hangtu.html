Zhuxi · north-western Hubei

Hangtu Heritage Park

The house is built of the earth itself: walls rammed from the raw soil, left with their natural grain, their pores, and the marks of time.

It is a plain and substantial kind of luxury, without pretence — still, and with a weight of its own. Underfoot are old blue bricks, worn and warm. To walk barefoot on them is to feel the house close to the ground, a retreat into the land rather than away from it.

On the Zhuxi intangible-heritage week you stay here, or at the lacquer-theme guesthouse in the International Lacquer Art Village, not at both. Breakfast is included. Dates are confirmed on request.

The rooms

The house offers singles, doubles, triples, family rooms for four, and family rooms for five or six. Further practical details, and reviews from earlier guests, can be read on Trip.com (Ctrip). We do not publish a room rate here.

The park is also a place of living crafts. A morning or an afternoon among the heritage workshops can be arranged as part of the week — the programme speaks of eight crafts; we will set out what is actually on offer once we know your dates.

On the Zhuxi week this is one of two houses, of equal standing. Back to the journey.
