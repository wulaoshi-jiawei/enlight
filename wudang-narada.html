Taiji Lake · Wudang Mountain, Hubei

Wudang Mountain Narada Resort

The resort stands on Taiji Lake, at the northern foot of the mountain and next to the scenic area itself — a World Heritage landscape of halls and hills, and a quieter Chinese elegance among the slopes and the water.

The house is built among the hills and the water. It is arranged in five parts — rooms, dining, banquets, leisure, and a wellness centre — and is meant as a restorative stay, away from the noise of the city. Rooms look out into the green of the hills; the table is seasonal and local.

The house is at No. 30 Taiji Lake East Road, in the Wudang Mountain Special Economic Zone. The official telephone number is +86 719 5668 888. It is about 22 kilometres from the centre of Shiyan, about 4.5 kilometres from the Fuyin Expressway exit for Wudang, and about 45 kilometres from the Danjiangkou Reservoir, the source of the South–North Water Transfer. Wuhan is about four hours by road; Xi’an and Zhengzhou about four and a half.

The house

The hotel was invested in by the Wudang Mountain Cultural Tourism Development Group, and is managed by Narada Hotels. It stands on about 135 mu, with a total building area of 60,000 square metres, and is intended as a leisure resort for travel, culture, and wellness.

There are 350 rooms and suites. An all-day restaurant and a Narada specialty restaurant serve seasonal local ingredients. The house has eight private dining rooms, two multifunctional banquet halls that can seat nearly a thousand, and nine meeting rooms. There is also a wellness centre, a KTV, a gym, a basketball court, and more than two hundred parking spaces.

We do not publish a rate here. Further practical details are on the official property page. On the 24 Solar Terms retreats you stay at this house or at Yuanhe Meisu, not at both. Breakfast is included when you stay here on the retreat.
